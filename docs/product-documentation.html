System Overview

The Online Vehicle Inspection Platform is a web-based system that allows drivers to get their vehicles inspected for ride-sharing companies (Uber, Lyft, Turo, and others). The system handles the entire inspection process digitally — from form submission, photo uploads, payment, to certificate generation.

Key Features

The platform includes the following core capabilities:

Multi-Company Support

Supports inspections for multiple ride-sharing platforms including Uber, Lyft, Turo, and more. Select which companies you need during the inspection process.

Digital Photo Upload

Upload required photos and videos directly through the browser. All files go to secure cloud storage for fast processing.

Instant Payment

Secure Stripe payment processing with real-time status updates. Multiple payment failure recovery options.

Automatic Certificates

Upon approval, certificates are automatically generated and saved to cloud storage, accessible for download at any time.

Getting Started

Account Creation

Creating an account is simple:

  1. Navigate to the login page
  2. Click "Sign Up" to create a new account
  3. Fill in your personal details (first name, last name, email, password, phone number)
  4. Your username will be automatically generated from your name and a unique identifier
  5. Click "Create Account" — you'll be automatically logged in

Logging In

Returning users can log in using either:

  • Email & Password — Standard credential-based login
  • Google OAuth — One-click login with your Google account
Note: If your session expires, you'll be automatically redirected to the login page. A helpful message will appear indicating your session has expired.

Your Dashboard

Your dashboard is the central hub for managing all your vehicle inspections. It serves different views depending on your role (customer, inspector, or administrator).

Customer Dashboard

When logged in as a driver, your dashboard shows:

  • Pending Inspections — Inspections awaiting review or payment
  • In Progress Inspections — Inspections being reviewed by inspectors
  • Approved Inspections — Completed inspections with certificates
  • Rejected Inspections — Inspections requiring corrections
Quick Actions: Use the "Add" or "Filter" buttons on the dashboard to create a new inspection or filter existing ones by status.

Navigation Header

The header navigation (at the top of every page) provides:

  • Language Selector — Switch between available languages (Not implented - UI only)
  • Brand Logo — Click to return to the homepage
  • Menu — Access to dashboard, login, and logout

Creating a New Inspection

Creating a new inspection is a 7-step process. Each step must be completed before moving to the next.

Step 1: Vehicle & Selection

Select your vehicle details and which companies you're inspecting for:

  • Enter your license plate number
  • Enter your vehicle's mileage
  • Select your country (USA or Canada)
  • Select your state/province from the dropdown
  • Choose which companies you need inspection for (checkboxes with company logos)
  • If Turo is selected, you'll see additional questions:
    • Are the tires less than 6 years old?
    • Is the battery less than 5 years old?
    • Is the voltage greater than 12.1V? (Only shown if battery is older than 5 years)

Step 2: VIN & License

Enter your vehicle's technical details:

  • VIN — 17-character vehicle identification number
  • Make — e.g., Toyota, Honda
  • Model — e.g., Camry, Accord
  • Year — select from a dropdown
  • Fuel Type — Gasoline, Diesel, Electric, Hybrid, or Hydrogen

Step 3-6: Media Uploads

Upload required photos and videos in 4 separate steps:

  • Step 3 — Media A — Registration card photo, odometer photo, horn video (5-second minimum)
  • Step 4 — Media B — Interior driver side, driver seat adjustment video, interior passenger side, passenger seat adjustment video
  • Step 5 — Media C — Interior back seat, exterior left side, exterior right side
  • Step 6 — Media D — Exterior front video, exterior rear video, and 4 tire photos (driver & passenger side, front & rear)
Upload Tips: Supported formats include JPG, PNG, HEIC, WebP, MP4, MOV, and more. Images should be under 50MB and videos under 200MB. Files upload directly to secure cloud storage — your browser handles the upload while you continue filling the form. You can proceed to the next step even while uploads are still processing.

Step 7: Review & Payment

Before submitting, review all your information:

  • Summary of your vehicle details and selected companies
  • Order total (varies by companies selected)
  • Digital signature via canvas
    • (Under Process)
  • Two required agreement checkboxes:
    • User Agreement — Terms of using the platform
    • Inspection Agreement — Confirmation all photos are accurate
Signature: Use the canvas to draw your signature with your mouse or finger (on touch devices). You can clear and redraw as needed.

Re-inspection Flow

If your inspection is rejected, you can re-inspect your vehicle without starting over. Re-inspection costs $0.00 — no additional payment is required.

How to Start a Re-inspection

You can start a re-inspection from two places:

  1. From the Inspection Card — When viewing your inspection list, click the chevron icon to unfold a card. If the status is "Rejected," you'll see a "Re-inspect Car" link.
  2. From the Certificate Panel — On the inspection detail page, if your inspection was rejected, a "Re-inspect Car" button is visible.

Re-inspection Steps

The re-inspection form mirrors the original 7-step process with key changes:

  • Step 1-2 — Your vehicle and VIN details are pre-filled and read-only (disabled). You cannot edit them.
  • Step 3-6 — All media must be freshly uploaded. You cannot reuse images from the previous inspection.
  • Step 7 — $0.00 pricing, signature canvas required, no payment form.
Important: After submitting a re-inspection, the inspection status changes to "In Progress" and your assigned inspector remains the same. You do not need to pay again. The original payment status is preserved.
Session Persistence: Your form data is saved in your browser's session storage. If you refresh the page or accidentally close the tab, your progress is preserved.

Payment System

All payments are processed securely through Stripe. The platform supports multiple payment scenarios:

Payment Scenarios

Initial Payment

When creating a new inspection, you pay the order total. Payment status starts as "pending" and becomes "paid" upon successful processing.

Payment Failure Recovery

If a payment fails, you can retry from the inspection card or detail page. The system tracks failed payments and shows helpful error messages.

Invoice Payment

If you receive an invoice link, you can pay through the dedicated "Pay Invoice" page without creating a new inspection.

Re-inspection (Free)

Re-inspections cost $0.00. No payment is required. Your original payment status is preserved.

Checking Payment Status

Your payment status is visible everywhere:

  • On the inspection card in your dashboard
  • On the inspection detail page
  • In the step 7 review/confirmation page
  • In the order summary

Payment Status Types

Status Description
Pending Payment initiated but not yet completed
Paid Payment completed successfully
Failed Payment attempt failed — retry available
Refunded Payment was refunded
Requires Action Payment needs additional confirmation (e.g., 3D Secure)
Processing Payment is being processed by the bank
Security Note: All payment processing happens securely through Stripe. The platform never stores your payment card information.

Managing Your Inspections

Inspections List

Your dashboard shows all inspections in a card layout. Each card displays:

  • Company logo(s) for the inspection
  • Vehicle information (VIN, make, model)
  • Status badge (Pending, In Progress, Approved, Rejected)
  • Payment status
  • Date created and last modified

Filtering & Searching

Use the filter bar to:

  • Filter by inspection status (all statuses)
  • Sort by date (newest or oldest first)

Inspection Detail View

Each inspection has a detail page showing:

  • Header — Status, handler name (if assigned), last modified date
  • Vehicle Specs — VIN, make, model, year, mileage, fuel type
  • Companies — Selected companies with logos
  • Media Gallery — All uploaded photos and videos organized in tabs
  • Certificates Panel — Shows generated certificates (if approved)
  • Rejection Info — If rejected, shows which fields were invalid and the rejection reason
Actions available depending on status:
  • Rejected — "Re-inspect Car" button appears
  • Pending Payment — "Payment Link" or "Retry Payment" appears
  • Approved — Certificates are available for download

Admin & Inspector Panel

Users with administrator or inspector roles have access to an admin panel at /dashboard/admin.

Admin Panel Pages

  • Requests — Inspections that are assigned to you and need review. Shows pending inspections awaiting your action.
  • Users — Manage user accounts (admin only). View all users, edit profiles, block/unblock.
  • Archive — Completed, approved, rejected, or cancelled inspections. Searchable and filterable.
  • Proposals — Inspection proposals (upcoming feature)
  • Settings — Company settings and preferences (upcoming feature)

User Management

Administrators can manage user accounts:

  • View all users (excluding other administrators)
  • Edit email, names, phone number, and role
  • See date joined and user registration details
  • Block or unblock users as needed

Inspection Approval Process

Inspectors and administrators can take action on inspections:

  • Approve — Approves the inspection, generates certificates, and notifies the customer
  • Reject — Sends the inspection back with specific fields marked as invalid and a reason
  • Assign Inspector — Admin can assign an inspection to a specific inspector
Rejection Details: When rejecting an inspection, the admin specifies which fields are invalid using a multi-select dropdown, and provides a reason. These details are visible to the customer on the inspection detail page, making it clear what needs correction.

Certificate Generation

When an inspection is approved:

  1. Certificates are automatically generated for each selected company
  2. Certificates are saved to cloud storage (Bunny CDN)
  3. PDF certificates can be downloaded from the inspection detail page
  4. Certificate URLs are permanently stored for future access

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to pay again for a re-inspection?

No. Re-inspections cost $0.00. Your original payment is preserved and no additional charge is made. After submitting a re-inspection, the status changes to "In Progress" while your inspector reviews the updated information.

How long does an inspection take?

After submission, inspections are typically reviewed within 1-2 business days. You'll see the status change on your dashboard. For urgent cases, contact support.

Can I edit my inspection after submitting?

Once submitted, inspections can only be modified through the re-inspection flow (if rejected). If your inspection is pending or in progress, you can use the "Save and Continue Later" feature — your progress is automatically saved in your session.

What file formats are supported for uploads?

Images: JPG, PNG, HEIC, WebP, BMP, TIFF (max 50MB each). Videos: MP4, MOV, AVI, WebM (max 200MB each). All uploads go directly to secure cloud storage.

Can I upload photos directly from my phone?

Yes! The upload system works on mobile browsers. You can take photos directly with your camera or upload from your gallery. Videos can also be recorded in-browser on mobile devices.

What happens if I'm rejected?

If your inspection is rejected, you'll receive an email notification and see the status update on your dashboard. On the inspection card, click the chevron to unfold details — the rejection reason will be visible. A "Re-inspect Car" link will appear, allowing you to start a new re-inspection with fresh media uploads.

How do I download my certificate?

Certificates appear in the Certificates Panel on your inspection detail page once approved. Each company has its own certificate link. Click to open the PDF, then use your browser's download button to save it locally.

Can I upload files in the background while continuing the form?

Yes. The platform supports background uploads — you can proceed to the next step while files are still uploading. Upload progress is shown as a percentage on each file.

Is my payment information secure?

Absolutely. All payment processing happens through Stripe's secure infrastructure. Your card details are never stored on our servers. The platform only receives payment status updates (paid, failed, etc.).

I forgot my password — how do I reset it?

On the login page, click "Forgot Password?" Enter your email address and you'll receive a password reset link.

Can I use this on mobile?

Yes — the platform is fully responsive and works on all devices including smartphones and tablets. The signature canvas is touch-compatible.
